**The year is 1916. High above the emerald Isonzo, two siege guns wake on
opposite ridges — the Austro-Hungarian Skoda 30.5 cm mortar and Italy's
Obice da 305. Between them lie limestone peaks, snow, drifting wind — and
mathematics.**

**SKODA 305** is a love letter to the oldest genre in computer gaming: the
artillery duel. From the text-mode *Artillery* games of 1970s mainframes to
the shareware legend *Scorched Earth* (1991), one idea has survived every
generation of hardware: two guns, one angle, one charge of powder, and the
wind laughing at your arithmetic. This game carries that torch back to
where the big guns really stood — the **Isonzo Front, 1915–1917**.

Built as if it were 1994: true **320×200 VGA**, a fixed palette, ordered
dithering, a chunky pixel font, 8-bit Sound Blaster crunch — every sprite,
sound and background generated from code, every photograph a real archival
plate from the war, rendered in sepia the way the era remembered itself.

### Features

- **Turn-based artillery duels** on fully destructible alpine terrain —
  craters bite, ridges collapse, guns fall
- **Wind that lives**: it drifts and gusts every shot; read the signal
  flag, listen to your soldier's estimate in knots, or switch on the
  **advanced ballistic calculator** and argue with physics directly
- **Two historical guns**, faithfully unequal: the Skoda 30.5 cm Mörser
  M.11 against the longer-reaching Obice da 305/17
- **1 player** against the prescription software, or **2 players** at one
  keyboard, first to three rounds
- **SCHOOL** — a field manual with the game's real equations, the wind flag
  lesson, and fact sheets with period photographs and verified
  specifications
- **Four languages**: English, Slovenian, German, Italian
- An original melancholic march in A minor, and silence where it matters

### In memoriam

This game is set on a real front. Between 1915 and 1917, twelve battles of
the Isonzo were fought through these mountains, and hundreds of thousands
of soldiers of many nations — Slovenes, Italians, Austrians, Hungarians,
Czechs, Croats, Bosnians, and more — never came home from the Isonzo.

This small game is played in their mountains. To all of them, on every
side of the wire: **respect, and eternal peace.**

*To all who fell on the Isonzo Front — eternal peace and remembrance.*

May the only shells that ever again fly over the Isonzo be made of pixels.

### Download, install & run

Every build is a single self-contained game — no installer, no
dependencies, nothing written outside your user folder.

**Play in the browser** — press **Run game** above. Keyboard required.

**Linux** — download the Linux zip, unzip, and run:

```
unzip skoda-305-*-linux-x86_64.zip && cd skoda-305* || true
chmod +x skoda-305-linux-x86_64
./skoda-305-linux-x86_64
```

Optional: `sh install.sh` puts SKODA 305 in your application menu with its
icon (installs to `~/.local`, no root needed).

**Windows** — download the Windows zip, right-click → *Extract All*, then
double-click `skoda-305-windows-x86_64.exe`. If SmartScreen objects to an
unsigned indie game, choose *More info → Run anyway*.

**macOS** — download the macOS zip, unzip, then **right-click
`Skoda 305.app` → Open** (needed once, because the app is unsigned).
Universal binary — runs on Apple Silicon and Intel alike.

Made with the wonderful free and open-source
**[Godot Engine 4.7](https://godotengine.org)**.
